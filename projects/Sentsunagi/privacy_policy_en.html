Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

日本語

kt-studio ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app ("the App") handles information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect any of your information.

The only data the App stores is the following game data, kept on your device and never transmitted to any server.

  • Which puzzles you have solved, and how long each took
  • An unfinished board and its elapsed time (so you can continue)
  • Your settings (showing numbers, line thickness, showing the timer)

We never collect personally identifiable information such as your name or email address. There is no account and no sign-in.

2. How the data is used

The game data stored on your device is used only to:

  • Provide the game itself (showing your records and resuming an unfinished puzzle)

3. Sharing with third parties

Because the App stores data only on your device and never transmits it, we do not share information with third parties, except where disclosure is required by law.

4. Advertising and tracking

The App shows no advertising. It does not access or use advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID), and it does not track you.

5. Network use

The App does not use the internet. All puzzles are bundled with the App, and no network connection is required to play.

6. In-app purchases

The App has no in-app purchases. Every feature is free.

7. Storage and deletion

  • Game data is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.
  • You can also erase it from Settings → "Erase records" inside the App.

8. Security

We manage the information we handle with appropriate security measures. However, no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. If we unintentionally collect information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as needed. If a significant change is made, we will announce it in the App or on this page.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the support link on the App's store page.
